# UNIRAS Language & Brand Grammar

Status: Canonical for public corporate surfaces.

## Purpose
UNIRAS communicates as a Malaysian technology company with an international B2B standard. English and Bahasa Melayu are written independently for natural professional usage; neither language is treated as a literal translation of the other.

## Reference hierarchy
1. Bahasa Melayu terminology: Dewan Bahasa dan Pustaka / PRPM terminology where a clear technology term exists.
2. English technology language: established software-engineering, product-engineering and open-source usage.
3. Domain accuracy: preserve an industry term in English when a forced translation would reduce precision or sound unnatural to a Malaysian technology audience.
4. Public readability: prefer plain corporate language over internal engineering shorthand.

## English grammar
- Use direct, specific B2B language: `build`, `operate`, `engineer`, `deploy`, `integrate`, `maintain`.
- Prefer `production-grade engineering` over vague phrases such as `production-minded engineering`.
- Avoid inflated claims: world-class, revolutionary, cutting-edge, best-in-class, industry-leading, enterprise-grade unless independently evidenced.
- Prefer `We build and operate technology products.` over startup-style promotional copy.
- CTA labels must be short verbs: `View products`, `Discuss a project`, `View platform`, `Request vendor documents`.

## Bahasa Melayu grammar
- Use professional Malaysian Bahasa Melayu, not word-for-word translation.
- Prefer `antara muka`, not `antaramuka`.
- Prefer `persepaduan sistem` when referring specifically to `system integration`.
- Prefer `pendaftaran vendor` over `onboarding vendor` on public procurement surfaces.
- Technical terms may remain in English where established software meaning would otherwise become ambiguous, for example `production`, API, PWA and SaaS.
- Avoid excessive English-Malay mixing within one sentence when an established Malay term is clear.

## Brand voice
UNIRAS should sound measured, capable and technically credible. The site should communicate engineering discipline without sounding like a developer portfolio, AI template, startup pitch deck or generic IT reseller.

## Visual grammar
- Deep navy/charcoal, white and cool neutral greys are the primary palette.
- Blue is an accent, not a decorative wash.
- No gradients, glow effects or exaggerated motion.
- Buttons use restrained radius, clear hierarchy and subtle state changes.
- Status labels are compact enterprise labels rather than rounded consumer-app pills.
- Procurement surfaces are the most conservative expression of the brand.
