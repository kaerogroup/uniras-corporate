## Rules

1. Keep the existing canonical URLs, historical indexing signals and sitemap entries. No unnecessary redirect or URL churn.
2. Do not create near-duplicate pages for each state or district merely to capture keyword variations. Build location-specific pages only with genuinely distinct local information and delivery proof.
3. Prefer native BM terminology plus industry-standard English terms (cold room, walk-in chiller/freezer) in natural sentences, not keyword stuffing.
4. Do not publish fabricated project counts, technical credentials, registered office locations, business ratings, or promised turnaround times.
5. Use Organization and Service structured data, with `areaServed` representing Terengganu/Kelantan/Pahang. Do not manufacture LocalBusiness addresses or Google Business Profiles for areas without a legitimate location and eligibility.
6. Measure qualified RFQs and WhatsApp enquiries by state/district; track confirmed serviceability and quote-to-close rate rather than raw clicks alone.
