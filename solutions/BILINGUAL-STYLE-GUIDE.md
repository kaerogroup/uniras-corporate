# UNIRAS Solutions — BM/EN Copy & Technical Terminology (v1.7)

## Policy
- Bahasa Melayu (BM, ms-MY) is the primary language for Malaysian customers.
- English (EN, en-MY) is business-facing Malaysian English. Use correct commercial refrigeration terminology, not literal word-for-word translation.
- UNIRAS coordinates commercial enquiries and connects clients with independent specialist vendors. Never claim UNIRAS directly installs, services, warrants, certifies or engineers projects.
- Avoid unverified price, certifications, delivery SLAs, capability claims or performance metrics.
- Maintain consistent spelling, capitalization and clarity in technical content.
